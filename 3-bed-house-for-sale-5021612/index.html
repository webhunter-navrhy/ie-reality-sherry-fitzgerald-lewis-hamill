Description

This fabulous three-bedroom end-of-terrace home is ideally located in the tranquil and highly sought-after Carraig Cluain development. Offering well-proportioned accommodation, a private rear garden and excellent convenience, this property is an ideal choice for first-time buyers, families or those looking for their forever home.

The accommodation comprises entrance hall, sitting room, kitchen/dining room and guest WC. Upstairs, there are three bedrooms, with the main bedroom benefiting from an ensuite, along with a family bathroom. Externally, the property features a private rear garden, perfect for enjoying outdoor dining and entertaining, together with convenient side access.

Located within walking distance of local schools, shops and Tullamore Hospital, the property enjoys a superb location close to all essential amenities. Viewing is highly recommended to fully appreciate this wonderful home.
